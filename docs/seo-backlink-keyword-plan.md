# IWIS Global: backlink and keyword plan (from the activities.marriott.com sheets)
Prepared 2 October 2026. Source: 110 backlink rows and 50 keyword rows for activities.marriott.com.

## 1. What the Marriott data really tells us
- Marriott's page is a resale shop (Hopper/PlacePass tours) inside a hotel loyalty brand. It ranks #2-#6 for Delhi-Agra searches only because of brand strength. IWIS is the real operator, so it can beat it on relevance, reviews and detail.
- About 85% of its backlinks are "Marriott Bonvoy Tours & Activities" mentions in points/credit-card articles. Those links come from the loyalty programme and cannot be copied. About 20 are spam. Only a few link types transfer to IWIS (section 3).
- 8 of the 50 keywords fit IWIS. The rest (Amritsar/Wagah, Kolkata tram, Goa, Pondicherry, Ayodhya, Hyderabad) are outside what IWIS sells.

## 4. Plan
### Weeks 1-2: foundations (cost: time only)
1. Verify the site in Google Search Console, submit the sitemap, request indexing for the tour pages (still open from earlier).
2. Rewrite title, H1, first paragraph and FAQ on the car-tour, train-tour and guided-Taj pages around the keywords above. Add TouristTrip/FAQ structured data.
3. Complete the Google Business Profile (hours 8 AM-10 PM IST, photos, tours, review link) and ask recent guests for Google reviews.
4. Create profiles with links to the site: TripAdvisor, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Pinterest (done), plus Indian tourism bodies you qualify for (check eligibility for UP Tourism approval and IATO/ATOAI membership).

### Weeks 3-6: linkable assets
5. Publish "Taj Mahal tour scams in Delhi and how to avoid them" (matches your positioning against fraudulent agencies).
6. Publish "Delhi to Agra in one day: car vs Gatimaan Express" with real timings, ticket prices and a map.
7. Publish a "Taj Mahal closed days, tickets and best time" reference page.
These are the pages other sites will link to.

### Weeks 4-12: outreach (target 4-8 quality links a month)
8. Hotel partnerships: contact 10-15 Agra/Delhi/Gurgaon hotels and serviced apartments for a listing on their experiences or concierge page.
9. Pitch the 7 travel publications and 3 trade outlets in section 3, one personalised email each.
10. Join journalist-request platforms (Connectively, Qwoted) and answer Taj Mahal and India travel queries.
11. Answer questions on TripAdvisor forums, Reddit r/IndiaTravel and Quora with the new guides (nofollow, but they bring buyers and AI citations).
12. Ask existing partners, drivers' associations and past guests with blogs for a link.

### Ongoing
13. Anchor text: about 60% brand (IWIS Global), 25% URL, 15% descriptive ("Taj Mahal private tour"). Never over-use the exact keyword.
14. Monthly: Search Console impressions and rank for the 8 keywords, new referring domains (DR 30+ only), and bookings from organic.

## 5. Timing and honest expectations
- It is early October and peak season runs Oct-Feb. New links take 2-3 months to affect rankings, so this season's gains come mostly from on-page work, Google Business Profile and reviews. Link building pays off mainly for next season.
- Long-tail terms (260-590 searches) can move in 3-6 months. The head term "delhi to agra taj mahal tour" needs 6-12 months and steady links; Viator, GetYourGuide and Marriott's resale page already sit there.
- The 5-8 links a month target is a goal, not a promise.
