**November through February** has the most comfortable daytime temperatures (roughly 22–29°C highs), but also the worst air quality of the year, with morning fog common enough to sometimes delay sunrise visibility. **October and March** are a middle ground: warmer, but clearer. There's a real trade-off here, not a single "best" answer. It depends whether you're optimizing for comfort or for a clear view.

## Month-by-month temperature

Averages below are 1991–2020 climate normals from the India Meteorological Department, via [Wikipedia's Climate of Agra data table](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_of_Agra).

| Month | Avg. high | Avg. low |
|---|---|---|
| January | 22.2°C | 7.4°C |
| February | 26.2°C | 10.4°C |
| March | 32.1°C | 14.2°C |
| April | 38.4°C | 20.0°C |
| May | 41.9°C | 24.4°C |
| June | 41.1°C | 25.5°C |
| July | 36.0°C | 24.7°C |
| August | 33.1°C | 23.9°C |
| September | 34.2°C | 23.5°C |
| October | 34.7°C | 18.7°C |
| November | 29.2°C | 13.2°C |
| December | 23.7°C | 8.1°C |

April through June regularly hits 40°C+ — outdoor walking on the marble platform in that heat is genuinely hard going, especially midday.

## The air quality trade-off winter doesn't advertise

This is the part most "best time to visit" pages skip. Agra's air quality gets measurably worse starting around October and stays elevated through winter — the same months that are most comfortable temperature-wise. ## Sunrise timing by season

Sunrise time matters directly for planning a sunrise tour's pickup time. Two confirmed anchor points for 2026, from [timeanddate.com](https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/india/agra):

- **Latest sunrise of the year:** 7:10 AM, around 12 January 2026 (winter)
- **Earliest sunrise of the year:** around the 21 June 2026 summer solstice (longest day, 13h 51m of daylight)

## So, when should you actually go?

| Priority | Best window |
|---|---|
| Coolest temperatures | December–January |
| Clearer air, still mild | October, March |
| Avoiding both extremes | Late October–November, or February–early March |
| Willing to trade comfort for lower crowds | May–August (hot, humid, fewer tourists) |
