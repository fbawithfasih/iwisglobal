If your India trip is already booked, or you are planning it yourself, you can add the Taj Mahal as **one private day** without changing the rest. Which option fits depends on where you will be: in Delhi, already in Agra, or travelling the Golden Triangle (Delhi, Agra, Jaipur).

## Which option fits your trip?

| Where you are | Option | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| In Delhi, with a free day | [Same-Day Tour by Car](/tours/same-day-agra-from-delhi) or [Gatimaan Express day tour](/tours/same-day-taj-mahal-tour-by-gatimaan-express) | Full day |
| In Delhi, wanting the Taj at sunrise | [Taj Mahal Sunrise Tour](/tours/taj-mahal-sunrise-tour) | Full day |
| Already in Agra | [Agra Private Sightseeing Tour](/tours/agra-private-sightseeing-tour): Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Baby Taj and Mehtab Bagh, from your hotel or station | Full day |
| In Agra in the evening | [Moonlight Night Viewing Tour](/tours/taj-mahal-moonlight-night-viewing-tour) on the official night-viewing nights | Evening |
| Want a night in Agra | [Agra Overnight Tour](/tours/agra-overnight-tour-from-delhi) | 2 days, 1 night |
| Want Jaipur as well | [3-day](/tours/3-days-golden-triangle-tour-from-delhi), [4-day](/tours/4-days-golden-triangle-tour-from-delhi) or [5-day](/tours/5-days-golden-triangle-tour-from-delhi) Golden Triangle by car | 3 to 5 days |

Every option is a private tour, with your own guide and vehicle rather than a shared group.

## What to check before you add a day

- **Friday:** the Taj Mahal is closed every Friday, so avoid that day ([opening hours](/guides/taj-mahal-opening-hours-closed-friday)).
- **Tickets:** Taj Mahal entry for a foreign adult is ₹1,300 (complex plus mausoleum); see the [ticket price guide](/guides/taj-mahal-ticket-price-2026). Check what each tour includes.
- **Season:** see the [best time to visit](/guides/best-time-to-visit-taj-mahal).
- **Timing:** one day is enough for the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort; see [Is one day enough?](/guides/is-one-day-enough-taj-mahal) and [car or train](/guides/delhi-to-agra-one-day-car-or-gatimaan-express).
