**Both work.** By car, the trip is about 230 km and roughly three hours each way on the Yamuna Expressway; by the **Gatimaan Express** it is 1 hour 40 minutes each way, but on a fixed timetable and not on Fridays. The car gives you control over the day; the train gives you a calmer, shorter ride. Either way, the Taj Mahal is **closed on Fridays**, so pick another weekday.

## At a glance

| | By car | By Gatimaan Express |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | about 230 km by road | about 188 km by rail (Delhi to Agra Cantt) |
| Time each way | about 3 hours (our schedule) | 1 hour 40 minutes |
| Timing | Your own: pickup around 5:30 AM on our tour, back in Delhi around 7:30 PM | Fixed: leaves Hazrat Nizamuddin 08:10, arrives Agra Cantt 09:50; returns 17:35, arrives 19:30 |
| Running days | Every day except Friday (Taj closed) | Every day except Friday |
| Extra costs | Expressway tolls: ₹415 one way for a car, included in a tour price | Train tickets (dynamic fares), booked on IRCTC or included in a tour |
| Flexibility | High: leave earlier, stay later | Low: you follow the train times |

Train times are from public timetable listings (checked 3 October 2026); confirm on [IRCTC](https://www.irctc.co.in/) before you book, because the railway revises timetables.

## Going by car

Central Delhi to Agra is about 230 km, 165 km of it on the [Yamuna Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamuna_Expressway) (speed limit 100 km/h for cars). Our [Delhi to Agra Same-Day Tour by Car](/tours/same-day-agra-from-delhi) leaves around 5:30 AM, reaches Agra around 8:30 AM, visits the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort with a guide, and is back in Delhi around 7:30 PM. For more comfort, the [Taj Mahal Luxury Car Tour](/tours/taj-mahal-luxury-car-tour-from-delhi) uses a Mercedes-Benz or BMW sedan for the same day. The early start is deliberate: visiting in the morning avoids the heat and the crowds ([tajmahal.gov.in FAQs](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/faqs.aspx)).

## Going by train

The Gatimaan Express leaves **Hazrat Nizamuddin**, not New Delhi station, and arrives at **Agra Cantt**. It does not run on Fridays. Our [Gatimaan Express day tour](/tours/same-day-taj-mahal-tour-by-gatimaan-express) includes executive-class train tickets both ways, entry to the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort, a private guide and a private vehicle in Agra. For the full timetable and booking steps, see our [Gatimaan Express guide](/blog/gatimaan-express-delhi-to-agra-timetable-fares-booking).

## Which should you choose?

- **You want the most time at the monuments, or a sunrise visit:** the car, or the [Taj Mahal Sunrise Tour](/tours/taj-mahal-sunrise-tour).
- **You dislike long road journeys:** the train, as long as you accept its fixed times.
- **You want to see more than the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort:** one day is tight; see [Is one day enough?](/guides/is-one-day-enough-taj-mahal) and consider the [2-day Agra overnight tour](/tours/agra-overnight-tour-from-delhi).
