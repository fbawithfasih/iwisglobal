The Taj Mahal **opens 30 minutes before sunrise and closes 30 minutes before sunset**, so the clock time changes with the season. **It is closed every Friday** for general viewing; on every other day it is open. Source: [ASI's official visiting-hours page](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/visiting-hours.aspx), checked 29 October 2026.

## Friday closure

ASI's page says the Taj Mahal "is closed on Fridays for general viewing. Other days it is always open." That means a same-day trip from Delhi cannot include the Taj Mahal on a Friday: plan Agra for another day. Agra Fort, Itimad-ud-Daulah and Fatehpur Sikri are open every day (see our [Agra attraction pages](/agra)), but the Taj Mahal is the reason most people come.

## Ticket counters and gates

- Ticket counters run from **1 hour before sunrise to 45 minutes before sunset**.
- Tickets are sold at the **West Gate** (near Saheli Burj) and the **East Gate**. The South Gate is exit-only at present.
- Foreign and domestic visitors use **separate queues**, with signs.
- Buying online is ₹50 cheaper per foreign ticket; see the [ticket price guide](/guides/taj-mahal-ticket-price-2026) for the full cost (₹1,300 for a foreign adult, complex plus mausoleum).
