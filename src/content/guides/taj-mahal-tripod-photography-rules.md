**No.** Tripods are explicitly listed as a prohibited item on [ASI's official "Dos and Don'ts" page](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/dos-and-donts.aspx) for the Taj Mahal, for regular day and night-viewing visitors alike. If you're planning to shoot with a tripod, plan on not bringing one in. Expect it to be turned away at the gate.

## What ASI's own rules say

The tripod ban sits in the same prohibited-items list as knives, chargers, and other electric goods — it isn't a separate, softer guideline. The same page says that whole list also applies to night viewing.

## Is there any way to get permission?

Yes — there is an official exception. A notice on [tajmahal.gov.in](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/) says: "Tripods are not allowed inside the Taj Mahal without proper permission issued from the Archaeological Survey of India."

What the official pages don't say is how that permission is granted: who qualifies, whether there's a fee, or which office handles it. Third-party sources (photography forums, tour-operator blogs) describe written permission for commercial or professional shoots, sometimes with a fee, but we can't confirm that from a primary source. If a tripod matters for your trip, contact [ASI Agra Circle](https://asiagracircle.in/contact-us.html) directly, well before your visit.

## What actually works instead

- A monopod is a different situation, and some visitors report far less friction using one — but it isn't explicitly addressed in ASI's published rules either. <!-- TODO(fasih): confirm monopod status directly rather than assuming it's allowed. -->
- Image stabilization on a modern phone or mirrorless camera handles most of what a tourist would have used a tripod for — sunrise light is workable handheld well before you'd need a long exposure.
- If a tripod shot genuinely matters to you (product photography, professional portrait work), that's the case to actually go through ASI's permission process for, well ahead of your visit date.

## Selfie sticks

Selfie sticks aren't on ASI's published prohibited list. Security staff have the final say at the gate, so if one matters to you, ask before you rely on it.

Tripods are one item on a longer prohibited list — see our [complete "what not to bring" guide](/guides/what-not-to-bring-taj-mahal) for everything else that's banned, or our [Taj Mahal attraction page](/agra/taj-mahal) for entry tickets and hours. If sunrise light for photos is the actual goal, IWIS Global's [Taj Mahal Sunrise Tour](/tours/taj-mahal-sunrise-tour) is timed for exactly that window.
