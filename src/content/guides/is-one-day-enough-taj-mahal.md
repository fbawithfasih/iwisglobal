**Yes, one day is enough** to see the Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, and get back to Delhi the same night — it's a well-worn route, roughly 230 km each way via the Yamuna Expressway, and our own Same-Day tour allows about three hours on the road each way. The real question isn't whether it's *possible* in a day; it's how much of that day you spend in the car versus at the monuments, which depends heavily on your departure time.

## Distance and route

Central Delhi to Agra is roughly **230 km**, **165 km** of it on the [Yamuna Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamuna_Expressway) from Greater Noida to Agra (speed limit 100 km/h for cars). A car pays **₹415 in tolls one way**, across three toll plazas — Jewar ₹120, Mathura ₹155 and Agra ₹140 — per the [Yamuna Expressway Authority's published rates](https://yamunaexpresswayauthority.com/About-Us/Infrastructure/yamuna-expressway/toll-rate-travel).

Here's the schedule for our own [Delhi to Agra Same-Day Taj Mahal Tour by Car](/tours/same-day-agra-from-delhi) — actual times shift a little with your pickup location, traffic and season:

| Time | What's happening |
|---|---|

Getting to the Taj in the morning is deliberate: the official Taj Mahal website notes that visiting early lets you "avoid the heat during summer but also the crowds", and that there are usually more visitors at sunset than sunrise ([tajmahal.gov.in FAQs](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/faqs.aspx)).

## Why departure time matters more than most guides mention

The Yamuna Expressway itself is fast and well-maintained, but travel time still varies with:

- **Time of day** — early morning departures generally avoid the worst of both Delhi's and Agra's local traffic on either end of the expressway
- **Season** — winter fog (see [our month-by-month guide](/guides/best-time-to-visit-taj-mahal)) can slow the early morning stretch specifically
- **Day of week** — the Taj Mahal is **closed every Friday**, so a Friday same-day trip isn't possible for the main monument

## What you actually get to see in a day

A realistic same-day itinerary covers the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort comfortably. Fatehpur Sikri or a longer, unhurried visit to each site would need an overnight stay — one day is enough to see the highlights, not to linger.

## Bottom line

One day works well if you depart early, accept that most of the day is either at the two main monuments or in the car, and don't try to add a third stop. It doesn't work if you want a slow, unhurried visit to more than two sites, or if your trip happens to fall on a Friday.
