Foreign tourists pay **₹1,100** for Taj Mahal complex entry, plus a separate **₹200** ticket to enter the main mausoleum chamber — **₹1,300 total** per adult. Indian nationals pay ₹50 (complex) + ₹200 (mausoleum); SAARC/BIMSTEC nationals pay ₹540 + ₹200. Children under 15 enter free, for both tickets. Source: [ASI's official ticketing page](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/ticketing.aspx), checked 27 September 2026.

## Why most answers stop at ₹1,100

₹1,100 is the price of complex entry — the gardens, the reflecting pool, the view of the monument from outside. It does **not** include the inner chamber beneath the central dome, where the cenotaphs of Shah Jahan and Mumtaz Mahal actually are. That's a separate ₹200 ticket, bought at a counter inside the complex. Most quick-answer sources (including AI Overviews summarizing older listings) quote the complex-entry figure alone and stop there, which understates what a visitor who wants to see the tomb chamber actually pays.

## The full cost stack (foreign tourist, 2026)

| Item | Price (₹) | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Complex entry | 1,100 | Yes |
| Main mausoleum ticket | 200 | Optional, but most visitors want it |
| **Total for full visit** | **1,300** | |

Indian nationals: ₹50 + ₹200 = ₹250. SAARC/BIMSTEC nationals: ₹540 + ₹200 = ₹740. Children under 15: free on both tickets, all nationalities.

## What's included in the foreign-tourist ticket

The ₹1,100 entry ticket bundles in a few things that aren't obvious from the price alone, per ASI's ticketing and Dos and Don'ts pages:

- Shoe covers
- A half-litre bottle of water
- A tourist guide map of Agra
- Battery bus and golf cart services

ASI's [Dos and Don'ts page](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/dos-and-donts.aspx) says SAARC and BIMSTEC tickets include the same. Separately, shoe racks just below the main mausoleum are free for every visitor, whatever their ticket.

## Online vs. counter: is there a price difference?

A small one. ASI's [visiting-hours page](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/visiting-hours.aspx) says tourists who buy tickets online get a discount of ₹50 per foreign ticket (₹5 per Indian ticket). Tickets are sold online through [asiagracircle.in](https://www.asiagracircle.in) and [tajmahal.gov.in](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in), and at the monument's West and East gate counters (1 hour before sunrise to 45 minutes before sunset); the South gate is exit-only for now. Foreign and domestic visitors queue separately, with signs. ASI's page lists no extra online service fee, so if an operator adds one, it is that operator's own markup.
