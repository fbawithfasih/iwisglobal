Foreign tourists pay **₹1,100** for Taj Mahal complex entry, plus a separate **₹200** ticket to enter the main mausoleum chamber — **₹1,300 total** per adult. Indian nationals pay ₹50 (complex) + ₹200 (mausoleum); SAARC/BIMSTEC nationals pay ₹540 + ₹200. Children under 15 enter free, for both tickets. Source: [ASI's official ticketing page](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in/ticketing.aspx), checked 21 September 2026.

## Why most answers stop at ₹1,100

₹1,100 is the price of complex entry — the gardens, the reflecting pool, the view of the monument from outside. It does **not** include the inner chamber beneath the central dome, where the cenotaphs of Shah Jahan and Mumtaz Mahal actually are. That's a separate ₹200 ticket, bought at a counter inside the complex. Most quick-answer sources (including AI Overviews summarizing older listings) quote the complex-entry figure alone and stop there, which understates what a visitor who wants to see the tomb chamber actually pays.

## The full cost stack (foreign tourist, 2026)

| Item | Price (₹) | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Complex entry | 1,100 | Yes |
| Main mausoleum ticket | 200 | Optional, but most visitors want it |
| **Total for full visit** | **1,300** | |

Indian nationals: ₹50 + ₹200 = ₹250. SAARC/BIMSTEC nationals: ₹540 + ₹200 = ₹740. Children under 15: free on both tickets, all nationalities.

## What's included in the foreign-tourist ticket

The ₹1,100 entry ticket bundles in a few things that aren't obvious from the price alone, per ASI's ticketing page:

- Shoe covers (required before entering the marble platform)
- A 500 ml bottle of water
- A tourist guide map
- Battery bus / golf cart transfer from the ticket counter to the gate
- Shoe rack access at the main mausoleum

## Online vs. counter: is there a price difference?

ASI's own ticketing page doesn't list a separate online-vs-counter price differential — tickets are sold both through [asiagracircle.in](https://www.asiagracircle.in) and directly on [tajmahal.gov.in](https://www.tajmahal.gov.in).
